Wild Rift Counter Assistant 3.4.9
====================================

Item icon identity fix:

- The item index no longer trusts the first image found in a WR Pocket card.
- Card images are accepted only when they are tied to the exact item detail link
  or their alt text matches the item slug.
- Every final Upgraded item icon is verified against that item's own detail page.
- The detail parser prefers the primary EquipIcons image around the H1 title and
  stops before Recipe components, preventing component/similar-item art from
  being assigned to the finished item.
- Item dataset schema is bumped to v4, forcing one fresh item refresh on existing
  installations so polluted icon URLs are replaced.
- When a verified icon URL changes, the media cache downloads it and replaces
  the stale PNG.

Known mismatches that exposed the bug included Harmonic Echo and several other
items whose cached icons did not match their names.
