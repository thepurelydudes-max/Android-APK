Wild Rift Counter Assistant 3.4.8
====================================

Final-item safety hardening:

- Build pools and counter-item pools are already filtered to WR Pocket tier=Upgraded.
- 3.4.8 adds an independent engine-level final gate before any item reaches the UI.
- Basic, Mid-tier and Starter-loadout recipe components are discarded from base,
  situational and ordered build outputs.
- Harmonic Echo / Мелодичное эхо remains allowed because it is a completed
  Upgraded support item in the current Wild Rift 7.3 item system.
